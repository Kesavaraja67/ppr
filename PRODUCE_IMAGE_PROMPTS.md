# Produce Product Photography Prompts & Image Guide

This document lists the **73 produce items** added to the PPR Fruits & Vegetables catalog along with the exact studio food photography prompt for each item.

---

## 📸 Image Guidelines for High Performance

- **Save Path**: `public/curated/<slug>.jpg` (or `.png` / `.webp`)
- **Dimensions**: Square 300x300 pixels (or 400x400)
- **File Compression**: Keep under **25 KB** per image using Web-optimized JPEG (quality 80-85%) or WebP.
- **Style**: Studio product photography on a clean white or soft neutral studio background, food styling, bright natural lighting.
